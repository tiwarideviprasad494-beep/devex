Freight explained simply

Sea or air? Full container or shared space?

FCL

Full Container Load gives your cargo a dedicated container. It can suit larger consignments.

LCL

Less than Container Load shares container space with other consignments, often suitable for smaller volumes.

Sea freight

Sea freight supports planned import and export movement across trade routes.

Air freight

Air freight can suit shipments where speed is important and cargo is appropriate for air carriage.

FAQ

Common questions, answered clearly.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL uses a dedicated container; LCL shares container space. The right option depends on volume, timing, route and handling needs.

Do you support first-time exporters?

Yes. We can help with export-related documentation preparation and first-time registration and KYC guidance.

Can you help with ICEGATE or port registration?

We provide guidance on relevant steps where applicable. Requirements and processes vary; Devex is not affiliated with ICEGATE or port authorities.

What details are needed for a quote?

Please share origin, destination, cargo type, freight mode, approximate weight or volume, ready date and relevant shipment requirements.

Which gateways and trade regions do you focus on?

We focus on coordination through JNPT and Mundra, including shipments involving the Middle East, Gulf and Africa.
